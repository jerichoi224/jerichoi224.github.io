Calendar house puzzle

Pick today's month, date and weekday — the solver arranges the 12 house pieces so only those three squares stay visible. Tap any square to uncover extras (weather, 日–土).

A faded chip means that piece isn't needed for this date (the 1×1 block rests in the frame's bottom notch when three squares show). Turn off flipping if your wooden pieces are painted on one side only.
